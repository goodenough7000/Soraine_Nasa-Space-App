Flame in Freefall

Same egg. Same pan. Different gravity.

About

A kitchen with no walls, sitting in a field, so the only thing that changes between scenes is the gravity the flame feels.

Each scenario pulses a wave out from the flame. What the wave has passed is already living under the new gravity; what it has not reached is still under the old one. The closer the wavefront is to you, the harder the camera shakes.

Flame shapes here are schematic: height and colour follow a simple buoyancy heuristic, not a combustion model. Figures in the detail page are labelled with their source or marked unverified.
