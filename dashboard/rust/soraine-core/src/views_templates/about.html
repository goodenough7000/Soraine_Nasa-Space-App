Reading the science

Published limits, fitted results, derived summaries and model-derived values are labelled separately. Experiment contexts are not independent trials. Pressure and oxygen describe the test environment, not a planet's outdoor atmosphere. The globe is illustrative and does not mark experiment locations. The microgravity view is an illustrative orbital laboratory, not a particular space station. Free fall can also be studied in drop towers. Its floating props do not simulate material fire behaviour. Lunar gravity is grouped nominally; individual test acceleration is described in each record.
