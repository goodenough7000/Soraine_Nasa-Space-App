# Visual and interaction handoff

The team direction is an interactive scene experience plus a research view, presented in a 30-second video. The familiar kitchen, stove, pan and egg introduce the experience. Ethan owns the Moon scene and Johnson owns Mars. The scene work and detailed transitions are separate from this contribution.

The finished research prototype provides a luminous orb, a command bar, a smooth transition into the Ultem Moon/Mars comparison, and an expanding evidence panel. The same surface returns to the originating scene with its selection preserved. Tony can use or adapt this interaction while defining the combined app's design.

## Included assets

- `navigator/public/media/orb-loop.mp4`: 512 × 512, 7.4-second muted loop, 407,676 bytes.
- `navigator/public/media/orb-poster.jpg`: still image for reduced motion.
- `navigator/src/Orb.jsx`: original procedural fallback if video cannot play.
- `navigator/design/orb-generation.json`: Higgsfield prompt, model, settings and generation job.
- `navigator/design/asset-manifest.json`: processing and asset details.
- `navigator/design/references.json`: credited design reference links.

The orb is decorative, not a combustion visualization. It uses a completed Higgsfield Seedance 2.5 generation from the unlimited allowance. Reduced motion uses a still image, and playback pauses in hidden tabs.

The earlier kitchen/orbit transition attempts and scene experiments are paused work. They are not shipped as finished scene modules. Reference artwork from other creators is linked rather than included as product assets.

When the teams combine the product, keep the exact sample identity and experiment conditions visible. A stove, egg or generic pan handle should not silently stand in for a tested Ultem film specimen. The shared information adapter already provides explanatory panels for those props.
