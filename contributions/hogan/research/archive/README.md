# Research archive

`soraine-master-research.json` is the original full merged research file, including contributor inputs, review decisions, original evidence, source catalogue, research gaps and storyboard history. It is kept unchanged for provenance, separate from the active scene information file.

`original-validation.json` records the normalization checks made during the original merge and the SHA-256 of that file. Its source input records distinguish Ethan's `soraine_research.json`, Tony's `idea_research_export.json`, Tony's separate validation report and Hogan's research handoff.

Historical local image paths in the archive identify the team's original drawings; they are not dependencies or public download links. The current visual handoff is [VISUAL-HANDOFF.md](../../VISUAL-HANDOFF.md). The archived storyboard does not override Tony's forthcoming design document.

The archive is not imported into the web app. Use `../soraine-information.json` and `../info-adapter.mjs` for scene panels.
